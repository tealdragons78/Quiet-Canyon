Quiet Canyon

Twenty loops of one hundred stages: canyon, rainforest, caves, ice and moonlight, then alien worlds, planets, and finally stars and suns. Finish a loop and the next one gets harder, with gaps sized for the upgrades you should own by then. Loop 20 is the last, and beating it brings a Rebirth.

Move with WASD or arrows. Jump with Space. Look by dragging, or with Q and E. Shift locks the mouse to your view. Double jump and extra air hops only work in the marked glowing-green zones. M mutes. Progress and coins save on this device.
